# Field visit notes: Kakuma community data sessions

*Fictional sample for testing. All names, numbers, addresses and IDs are made up.*

**Organisation:** Northbridge Relief Network (fictional)
**Visit dates:** 3 to 6 March 2026
**Location:** Kakuma refugee camp, Turkana County, Kenya
**Written by:** Amina Wanjiru, protection officer (amina.wanjiru@example.org, +254 700 555 012)

## Purpose

We held four sessions with community members about a planned chatbot that answers questions on food distribution and health services. The chatbot would be trained partly on anonymised transcripts of past helpline calls. We wanted to know whether people would agree to their calls being used, and on what terms.

## What we heard

- Most participants were willing to share their call transcripts if their names, phone numbers and camp block numbers were removed first.
- Several older participants did not understand what "training a model" means. Our interpreter, Joseph Ekai, explained it as "teaching the computer by showing it many examples". After that explanation, more people agreed.
- Three women asked that calls about gender-based violence never be used, even if anonymised. We agreed to exclude that category entirely.
- Young people asked whether they could withdraw later. We said yes, until the dataset is frozen on 30 June 2026.
- One participant, Grace Lokwang (phone +254 711 555 304), asked to receive a copy of the final consent form in Swahili.

## Consent approach agreed

1. Verbal consent recorded at the start of each helpline call, in the caller's language.
2. An opt-out by SMS to a short code, valid until the dataset is frozen.
3. No use of calls about gender-based violence, child protection or medical emergencies.
4. Names, phone numbers, block numbers and case IDs removed before any data leaves the field office.
