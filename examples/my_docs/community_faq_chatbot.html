Helpline chatbot: questions and answers

Northbridge Relief Network (fictional) is testing a chatbot that answers questions about food distribution, water points and health services. This page explains how your information is used.

Is the chatbot a person?

No. It is a computer program. It can make mistakes. For urgent help, call the helpline and ask for a staff member.

Will my calls be used to teach the chatbot?

Only if you agree. At the start of a call, the helpline worker will ask you. You can say no and still get the same help.

What do you remove before using a call?

We remove your name, phone number, camp block, case number and any medical details. Calls about violence, child protection and medical emergencies are never used.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. Send the word STOP by SMS to the short code 5050 before 30 June 2026. After that date the training data is frozen, but your call is never shown to anyone outside the project.

Who can I talk to?

Ask for the protection officer at the community centre, or write to privacy@example.org. You can also call the data protection lead, Laura Bianchi, on +41 22 555 01 87.

Does the chatbot share my answers with the government or other groups?

No. The chatbot does not send your questions to any authority. Northbridge Relief Network works independently of all parties to the conflict.
